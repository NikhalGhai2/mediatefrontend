05Security & data protection

Client confidentiality was the first requirement, not the last.

Family mediation handles some of the most sensitive material in law. Mediate was architected around that from the first line, with data handling designed to comply with UK GDPR.

Residency

UK and EU data residency

Case data is stored in a UK or EU region. Documents, transcripts and mail stay inside your own Microsoft or Google tenancy.

Access

Role-based access control

Row-level security across every case table, with distinct principal, administrator and auditor roles. Anonymous access is revoked outright.

Audit

Append-only audit log

Every read, draft and approval is recorded and cannot be edited or deleted by ordinary users — administrators included.

Training

No training on your data

Client material is never used to train models. It is processed to do the job you asked for, and nothing beyond it.

Isolation

Cross-party isolation

Each party is handled in a separate, structurally enforced pass. Party A's answers cannot leak into Party B's file.

Compliance

Built to GDPR regulations

Data handling is designed to comply with UK GDPR and EU GDPR: UK or EU residency, access controls, retention limits and a full audit trail.
