Built around client confidentiality.

Family mediation involves some of the most sensitive information in law. Mediate was designed around that from the start, with data handling designed to meet UK GDPR.

UK and EU data storage

Case data is stored in the UK or EU. Documents, transcripts and email stay in your own Microsoft or Google account.

Role-based access

Separate roles for principals, administrators and auditors, with access controlled on every case record.

A log that can't be changed

Every read, draft and approval is recorded. Nobody can edit or delete the log, administrators included.

Your data isn't used for training

Client material is only used to do the work you asked for. It is never used to train AI models.

Parties kept apart

Each party is handled separately, so one party's answers can't end up in the other's file.

Designed for UK GDPR

UK or EU storage, access controls, retention limits and a full audit log.
